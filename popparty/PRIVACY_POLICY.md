# Privacy Policy for Pop Party

**Last updated: December 2025**

## Overview

Pop Party ("we", "our", or "the app") is a casual mobile game designed for all ages. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
- We do not require account creation or login
- We do not access your contacts, photos, or files
- We do not track your location

### Information That May Be Collected
- **Game Progress**: High scores and settings are stored locally on your device only
- **Advertising Data**: Our advertising partners may collect anonymous device identifiers for ad personalization (see Advertising section)
- **Analytics**: Basic anonymous usage statistics may be collected to improve the game

## Advertising

Pop Party displays advertisements through Unity Ads. These ads help keep the game free.

Our advertising partner may collect:
- Device type and operating system
- Anonymous advertising identifiers
- General location (country/region, not precise location)

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings:
- **Android**: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Pop Party is designed to be appropriate for all ages, including children under 13. We:
- Do not knowingly collect personal information from children
- Display only age-appropriate advertisements
- Do not have social features, chat, or user-generated content

## Data Storage

All game data (scores, settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device. We do not have servers that store your personal data.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Unity Ads**: For displaying advertisements ([Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy))

## Your Rights

You can:
- Delete all game data by uninstalling the app
- Reset your advertising identifier in device settings
- Opt out of personalized advertising in device settings

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
